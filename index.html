Kumon Math Program

Develop and build critical math skills from counting through calculus. Students gain fluency, accuracy, and the confidence to tackle advanced concepts.

  • Counting, addition, subtraction, multiplication & division
  • Fractions, decimals, algebra, geometry
  • Trigonometry, calculus, statistics & probability
  • From kindergarten readiness to college entrance exams

Kumon Reading Program

How It Works

A simple path to independence

The Kumon Method is designed so students become self-learners—able to tackle new challenges on their own.

1

Free Assessment

We evaluate your child’s current level and recommend the ideal starting point—no obligation to enroll.

2

Individualized Plan

Your child receives a customized worksheet sequence that fills gaps and builds mastery at the right pace.

3

Daily Practice + Center

Just 30 minutes a day at home, plus two short center sessions each week with instructor guidance.
